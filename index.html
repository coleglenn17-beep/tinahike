COACHING NOTES — FROM YOUR TRAINER
▲
Every session starts with either sled work or stair climber — this directly mimics the demands of hiking uphill with a pack. Don't skip the cardio openers.
▲
Single leg exercises are the priority here. The hike involves uneven terrain where each leg works independently — Bulgarian split squats and single leg RDLs are your most important lifts.
▲
Calves get 25 reps every single session. Calf fatigue is the number one complaint on long hikes — we're building the endurance base now so you don't feel it on the trail.
▲
Week 2 increases load, incline percentage and cardio duration across every session. If Week 1 felt manageable, Week 2 should feel like a proper push.
▲
The incline treadmill goes from 8% in Week 1 up to 12% in Week 2. No holding the rails — that defeats the purpose and reduces the load on your legs and calves.
▲
Abs rotate each session — hanging knee raises, Swiss ball work, cable rotations and Pallof press. Core stability is essential for carrying a pack on uneven ground.
▲
Rest 90 seconds between working sets. Keep moving — 60 minutes goes quickly with the cardio blocks included.
▲
You've got this Tina. Your trainer will be checking in — enjoy the hike! 🏔️
